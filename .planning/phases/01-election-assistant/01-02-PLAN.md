Section header + 3 tabs (Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, Vidhan Sabha). Each tab contains:

**Lok Sabha tab:**
- Title: "Lok Sabha — House of the People"
- Visual process flow: Voter → EVM → Counting → Results → Government Formation
- Key facts cards:
  - 543 elected seats + 2 nominated (Anglo-Indian, discontinued)
  - FPTP voting system — candidate with most votes wins
  - Direct election by citizens 18+ years
  - 5-year term, can be dissolved early
  - ECI conducts elections using EVMs with VVPAT

**Rajya Sabha tab:**
- Title: "Rajya Sabha — Council of States"
- Key facts:
  - 245 members (233 elected + 12 nominated by President)
  - Indirect election — elected by MLAs using STV
  - 6-year term, 1/3 retire every 2 years
  - Permanent body — cannot be dissolved
  - Represents states and UTs at the federal level

**Vidhan Sabha tab:**
- Title: "Vidhan Sabha — State Legislative Assembly"
- Key facts:
  - Each state has its own assembly (60-403 seats)
  - FPTP voting (same as Lok Sabha)
  - Direct election by state citizens
  - 5-year term
  - Party with majority forms state government (CM)
  - Governor appoints CM
  - Currently ~4,120 MLA seats across India

Each tab content should use glass cards in a grid layout with inline SVG icons. Include a "How Voting Works" visual flow using CSS flexbox with step indicators.

<read_first>

</read_first>
<action>
Replace empty `<section class="faq-section" id="faq">` with accordion containing 10 questions:

1. "Who can vote in Indian elections?" — Indian citizen, 18+, registered in electoral roll, valid voter ID (EPIC) or approved ID
2. "What is the Election Commission of India (ECI)?" — Independent constitutional body (Article 324), conducts all elections, headed by CEC + 2 ECs
3. "How does the FPTP voting system work?" — Candidate with most votes wins, no minimum threshold needed, used in LS and VS elections
4. "What is NOTA and how does it work?" — None Of The Above, option on EVM since 2013, if all NOTA the candidate with most votes still wins
5. "How is the Prime Minister elected?" — Not directly; PM is leader of majority party/coalition in Lok Sabha, appointed by President
6. "What's the difference between Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha?" — LS: direct election, 543 seats, money bills, 5yr. RS: indirect by MLAs, 245 seats, review chamber, permanent
7. "What is an EVM and VVPAT?" — Electronic Voting Machine for digital voting, Voter Verified Paper Audit Trail prints receipt for verification
8. "How are constituencies delimited?" — Delimitation Commission redraws boundaries based on census data, reserved seats for SC/ST
9. "What is the Model Code of Conduct?" — Rules during election period, no government announcements, equal media, no inducements
10. "What happens during a hung parliament?" — No party gets 272+ seats, President invites largest party/coalition to form government, floor test

Each FAQ item: unique `id="faq-1"` through `id="faq-10"`, `data-searchable`, button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, answer panel with `role="region"`, chevron SVG.

CSS: `.faq-item`, `.faq-question`, `.faq-answer` with max-height transition, `.faq-chevron` rotation, `.faq-item.active` states. Layout in a max-width 800px centered column.
