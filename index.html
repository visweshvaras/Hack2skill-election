Understand the three main pillars of India's legislative system.

Lok Sabha — House of the People

Seats

543 elected seats

Voting System

First-Past-The-Post (FPTP) — candidate with most wins

Election

Direct election by citizens 18+ years

Term

5-year term, can be dissolved early

Mechanism

ECI conducts elections using EVMs with VVPAT

Government

Party/coalition with 272+ seats forms government

PM elected by majority party/coalition

Rajya Sabha — Council of States

Seats

245 members (233 elected + 12 nominated)

Election

Indirect election — elected by MLAs using STV

Term

6-year term, 1/3 retire every 2 years

Permanence

Permanent body — cannot be dissolved

Role

Represents states and UTs at the federal level

Vidhan Sabha — State Legislative Assembly

Seats

Each state has its own assembly (60-403 seats)

Voting System

First-Past-The-Post (FPTP) voting

Election

Direct election by state citizens

Term

5-year term

Government

Party with majority forms state government

Leadership

Governor appoints Chief Minister (CM)

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Indian democracy.

An Indian citizen, 18+ years of age, registered in the electoral roll, with a valid voter ID (EPIC) or an ECI-approved ID.

An independent constitutional body (Article 324) that conducts all elections, headed by the Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) and 2 Election Commissioners.

The candidate with the most votes wins. No minimum threshold is needed. This system is used in Lok Sabha and Vidhan Sabha elections.

NOTA stands for "None Of The Above". Available on EVMs since 2013, it allows voters to reject all candidates. However, even if NOTA gets the highest votes, the candidate with the next highest votes still wins.

The Prime Minister is not directly elected by the people. The PM is the leader of the majority party or coalition in the Lok Sabha, appointed by the President.

Lok Sabha has direct elections, 543 seats, handles money bills, and has a 5-year term. Rajya Sabha is elected indirectly by MLAs, has 245 seats, acts as a review chamber, and is a permanent body.

An Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) is used for digital voting. Voter Verified Paper Audit Trail (VVPAT) prints a paper receipt visible to the voter for 7 seconds to verify their vote before dropping into a sealed box.

The Delimitation Commission redraws constituency boundaries based on the latest census data to ensure roughly equal population representation, including allocating reserved seats for SC and ST communities.

It's a set of guidelines issued by the ECI regulating political parties and candidates during the election period. It prohibits new government announcements, hate speech, and voter inducements.

When no party or pre-poll alliance secures a clear majority (272+ seats), the President invites the leader of the single largest party or coalition to form the government and prove their majority in a "floor test".
