Voter Registration

The foundation of a democracy is its voters. In India, anyone who is a citizen and above 18 years of age is eligible to vote, provided they are registered in the electoral roll.

Form 6

The application form to register as a new voter. Can be filled online via the ECI portal or offline.

EPIC Card

Electors Photo Identity Card. Your official ID issued by the Election Commission to cast your vote.

BLO Verification

A Booth Level Officer will visit your address to verify your residency before issuing the card.

⚖️

Code of Conduct & Nominations

Once the Election Commission announces the dates, the Model Code of Conduct (MCC) kicks in. Politicians then officially step forward to run for office.

Model Code of Conduct

Rules that prevent the ruling party from misusing government machinery or announcing new policies to sway voters.

Filing Nominations

Candidates submit their nomination papers to the Returning Officer along with a security deposit.

Affidavits

Crucial step! Candidates must publicly declare their criminal records, assets, liabilities, and educational qualifications.

🎤

The Campaign Trail

This is the most visible phase of elections. Parties and candidates have about two weeks to reach out to voters and convince them.

Manifestos

Official documents released by political parties detailing their promises and policies if elected.

Expenditure Limits

The ECI sets strict limits on how much a candidate can spend on their campaign to ensure a level playing field.

The 48-Hour Silence

All public campaigning must stop 48 hours before voting begins to give voters time to think peacefully.

🗳️

Voting Day Process

The day millions exercise their democratic right. India uses Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) to ensure fast and secure polling.

Indelible Ink

A special ink mark is applied to the voter's left index finger to prevent them from voting twice.

EVM & VVPAT

You press a button on the EVM, and the VVPAT prints a slip showing your vote for 7 seconds to verify accuracy.

NOTA

None Of The Above. A button allowing voters to officially reject all contesting candidates.

📊

Results & Government Formation

After voting concludes across all phases, the EVMs are secured in strong rooms until Counting Day.

Counting Day

EVMs are unsealed in the presence of candidate representatives. Results are declared constituency by constituency.

The Magic Number: 272

In the Lok Sabha (543 seats), a party or coalition needs at least 272 seats to form a majority government.

Oath Taking

The President invites the majority leader to form the government and takes oath as the Prime Minister.
